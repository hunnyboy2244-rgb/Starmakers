About StarMakers

StarMakers is a website dedicated to upcoming and independent musicians, especially emerging African/Ghanaian artists. The goal is to give talented musician, visibility and practical support before they become established stars

Functions of StarMakers

Artist Registration-Upcoming musicians can create profiles and introduce themselves.
Artist Profiles- Display artist biography, genre,location,photos and music.
Music Submission-Artist can submit their songs for promotion and review.
Artist Discovery- Vistors can search and discover new musicians.
